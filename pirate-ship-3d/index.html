Ahoy, Captain!

  • Steer: WASD or the arrow keysthe joystick, bottom-left to sail, hold ShiftBOOST for a burst of speed.
  • Fight: SpaceFIRE to broadside — pull up alongside an enemy so your port/starboard cannons bear. Ramming at speed damages you both, too. Other captains are fair game anywhere outside port.

Know your shot

The rack along the bottom of the screen loads a different shot. They are not cosmetic — each one trades damage for something else.

  • Round — full damage, normal reload. The dependable broadside; use it when nothing cleverer is called for.
  • Chain — half damage and a slower reload, but it shreds their rigging: their top speed drops to a third for 3.5 seconds. The way to stop a captain with a fat hold from reaching port.
  • Grape — 60% more damage point-blank, 40% less at any real range. Rewards sailing right alongside.
  • Fire — only 40% on impact, then burns for 70% more over the next 4 seconds. The most total damage of any shot, but only if they live long enough to burn — and it finishes a fleeing ship you can no longer hit.
  • Your hold: gold you earn at sea is unbanked — sink and most of it spills into the water for anyone to scoop up. Sail into home port to bank it permanently. Banked gold is safe forever, and it's the only gold the shipyard takes.
  • Port is a sanctuary: nobody can be hurt inside the ring around home port, in either direction. Sail into it to bank, repair your nerve, and cool off.
  • Shipyard: sail back to home port (the gold marker on your minimap) to upgrade your ship and place cannons.
  • Treasure: sinking enemies sometimes drops a torn map — follow the glowing beacon to dig it up. It goes straight in your hold, so get it home.
  • Heat: sinking ships raises your wanted meter — sinking captains raises it far faster. High heat draws hunters and makes every bot around prefer you. Let it cool at port.
  • Chat: press Enter or tap the chat icon to talk to other captains.
