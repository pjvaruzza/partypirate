Ahoy, Captain!

  • Steer: WASD or the arrow keysthe joystick, bottom-left to sail, hold ShiftBOOST for a burst of speed.
  • Fight: SpaceFIRE to broadside — pull up alongside an enemy so your port/starboard cannons bear. Ramming a bot at speed damages you both, too.
  • Shipyard: sail back to home port (the gold marker on your minimap) to upgrade your ship and place cannons.
  • Treasure: sinking enemies sometimes drops a torn map — follow the glowing beacon to dig it up.
  • Heat: sinking ships raises your wanted meter. Let it cool at port before it draws a hunter your way.
  • Chat: press Enter or tap the chat icon to talk to other captains.
